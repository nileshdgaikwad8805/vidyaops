Frequently Asked Questions

What does VidyaOps offer?

We offer practical tech training in Cloud, Data Analysis, AI, and Cybersecurity through workshops, guided tracks, and certification programs.

Are there free workshops?

Yes. We offer free introductory workshops to help you explore a domain before committing to a paid training track.

Who can join?

College students, freshers, early professionals, and anyone looking to build practical tech skills. No prior experience is needed for beginner workshops.

Are the workshops online?

We offer both online and in-person sessions in Pune, Maharashtra. Check individual workshop details for format.

How do I enroll?

Visit our Enroll page or contact us for guidance on the right program for you.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, paid programs include a certificate of completion. Free workshops include a participation certificate.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments are processed securely via Razorpay using UPI, debit/credit cards, and net banking.
