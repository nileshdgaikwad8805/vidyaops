Applications Open: R&D Internship Program

Do you have an innovative idea you'd like to explore under industry guidance? VidyaOps offers a 3-Month Research & Development Internship Program for students, graduates, and early-career professionals who want to work on meaningful research, experimentation, and technology-driven projects with real-world impact.

This is not a shadowing program — you will own a research domain, work with emerging technologies, and produce tangible deliverables including documentation, prototypes, and research outputs that strengthen your professional portfolio.

How it works

From application to research output — a structured 3-month journey.

01

Apply & Get Selected

Submit your resume and research interest through email. Our team reviews applications, conducts brief discussions to understand your goals, and selects candidates who demonstrate genuine curiosity and initiative.

02

Research Planning & Mentorship

Once onboarded, you'll work with your assigned mentor to define research objectives, create a project plan, and establish milestones — ensuring your internship has clear direction and measurable deliverables.

03

Research & Development

Spend 3 months deeply engaged in your research domain — experimenting, building prototypes, analyzing results, and iterating on solutions with regular mentor check-ins and progress reviews.

04

Deliver & Certify

Complete your research with proper documentation, present findings, and receive your internship certificate. Strong research outputs may lead to publication opportunities or extended collaboration.
