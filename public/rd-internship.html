Applications Open: R&D Internship Program

Do you have an innovative idea you'd like to explore? Are you interested in conducting research under the guidance of an industry-focused organization?

VidyaOps is offering a 3-Month Research & Development (R&D) Internship Program for students, graduates, and early-career professionals who want to work on research, experimentation, innovation, and technology-driven projects.

3 Months Remote Unpaid Mentorship

Who Can Apply?

  • College Students
  • Recent Graduates
  • Early-Career Professionals
  • STEM Students

Program Details

Duration, Mode & Stipend

Duration

3 Months

Structured research internship with defined milestones and deliverables.

Mode

Remote

Work from anywhere with regular mentor check-ins and collaboration sessions.

Stipend

Unpaid
